Fantasy football for leagues where the waiver wire is empty

Most fantasy advice is written for 10- and 12-team leagues, where a decent starter is always one click away. In a 14- or 16-team league that stops being true, and the advice stops working with it. The Weekly Snap writes for the deep-league manager: weekly rankings, waiver adds, streaming picks, and plain-English strategy built around thin benches and thin waiver wires.

Start with the rankings and start-sit posts if you need a lineup answer this week, or read the analysis and takes if you want to understand why deep leagues play differently. Building a roster or weighing a trade? Check the top 150 PPR fantasy football rankings, tiered for 14- and 16-team leagues.
