Analysis and takes

Rankings tell you who to start. Analysis explains how to build a team that makes those choices easier. These longer pieces cover how deep leagues differ from standard ones, how to spend a waiver budget when every manager is hunting for the same handful of players, and how to get through bye weeks without a collapse.

The numbers in these posts come from simple league math, and the opinions are labeled as opinions.
