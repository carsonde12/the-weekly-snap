Four teams are on bye this week, the Bengals, Lions, Dolphins, and Vikings, which makes a deep-league waiver wire thinner than usual. The six players below are ranked by how much I would pay for them in a 14- or 16-team league. Roles and injury statuses are current through Wednesday, October 14, and they can change before kickoff.

## The six adds, in order

1. **Germie Bernard, WR, Steelers.** Bernard caught six of nine targets for 80 yards last week and looks like a fit for the slot. If Roman Wilson is out, as injury reports suggest, his route volume should hold. He is a PPR flex with a path to more.
2. **Dontayvion Wicks, WR, Eagles.** Wicks has scored in double figures in three of five games. He faces a Panthers secondary that reports describe as missing both starting cornerbacks, and DeVonta Smith is listed as questionable, which would push Wicks up the depth chart.
3. **Emmett Johnson, RB, Chiefs.** Johnson is the handcuff to Kenneth Walker III, who has logged 99 touches through four games and has a history of injuries. If Walker goes down, Johnson is an immediate starter. Add him before that happens, since the price changes the moment it does.
4. **Kendrick Bourne, WR, Cardinals.** Bourne is now Arizona's third option in the passing game and saw eight targets in Week 5. He is a PPR flex play for managers who need a body, not a ceiling.
5. **Jameis Winston, QB, Giants.** Winston finished as the sixth-highest scoring quarterback last week. In a deep league his best use is as a bye-week fill-in or a second quarterback, one week at a time.
6. **Samaje Perine, RB, Bengals.** Perine scored 15.8 fantasy points on 16 touches last week, but the Bengals are on bye in Week 6, so he cannot help you until Week 7. Stash him only if you have an open spot.

## What I would not chase

Do not bid on a player because he scored twice last week on four touches. In a deep league you need roles that repeat, and a touchdown spike does not. If a player's snap share and targets did not move, his price should not move either.
