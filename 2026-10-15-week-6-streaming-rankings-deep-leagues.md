Streaming in a 16-team league means picking from whoever is left, and with four teams on bye this week there is less left than usual. These rankings cover tight ends and defenses, the two positions where deep-league managers most often end up with an empty slot. Information is current through Wednesday, October 14.

## Tight ends

The two highest-ranked tight ends this week, Brock Bowers against Buffalo and Trey McBride at the Rams, are rostered in nearly every league, so they are not streamers. The three below are the realistic wire options. If your tight end is on bye, any of them fills the slot.

1. **Pat Freiermuth, Steelers.** Freiermuth scored twice last week and is averaging about twelve fantasy points a game. Pittsburgh plays Tampa Bay this week, then New Orleans and Cleveland, a run of matchups that look favorable on paper.
2. **Brenton Strange, Jaguars.** Strange has seen eight targets in each of his last two games and led the team in receptions over that span. Jacksonville hosts Houston, and target volume is the most reliable thing a streaming tight end can have.
3. **Hunter Henry, Patriots.** Henry had his first top-12 week of the season last week, with a touchdown, and New England plays the Jets. He is the lowest of the three because his production has been less steady.

## Defenses

Defense is the hardest position to stream in a deep league because the best units are usually owned. Weekly consensus rankings put the Chiefs, who host the Chargers, and the Seahawks, who visit Denver, at the top this week. If either is still available, add one immediately.

1. **Chiefs vs. Chargers.** The top-ranked defense this week, if you can get it.
2. **Seahawks at Broncos.** A close second, with the same caveat about availability.
3. **Steelers vs. Buccaneers.** Reports as of October 14 list Baker Mayfield as likely out with a thumb injury. A defense facing a backup quarterback is worth a look, and this is the most likely wire option of the three.

## How to stream a defense in a deep league

- **Start with the opponent's quarterback.** A backup or an injured starter matters more than the defense's reputation.
- **Prefer turnovers and sacks to yards allowed.** Fantasy defenses score on takeaways, not on stopping drives.
- **Do not chase last week's points.** A defense that scored 20 points last week often did it against a bad offense, and this week's opponent is what you are buying.
