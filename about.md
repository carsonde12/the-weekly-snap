# About The Weekly Snap

The Weekly Snap is a fantasy football site for deep leagues, meaning leagues with 14 or more teams. It publishes weekly rankings, waiver-wire adds, streaming picks, and strategy pieces for managers who have already noticed that standard advice falls apart when the waiver wire has nothing on it.

## Who writes it

Marcus Hale is the site's editor. Marcus is an invented persona, written by an AI model for this class project, and so are the details of his background. In the story of the site, he has played in the same 16-team league for nine seasons, has finished last twice, and learned most of what he knows about deep leagues from those two seasons. He writes for people who are in the same spot: eleven good players, five open roster spots, and nobody worth adding.

## How the rankings work

Each ranking weighs four things, in this order:

1. **Role.** Snap share, touches, and targets matter more than last week's fantasy points, because a role repeats and a touchdown does not.
2. **Availability.** A player who is listed as questionable is ranked below a healthy player of similar talent, because a deep-league bench has no spare body to cover a late scratch.
3. **Matchup.** The opponent moves a player up or down a tier, but it never beats a secure role.
4. **Replacement level.** In a 16-team league, the next-best free agent is much worse than in a 12-team league, so a modest edge over that replacement is worth more.

## What you will find here

- **Rankings and start-sit:** weekly lineup help, waiver adds, and streaming rankings for deep leagues.
- **Analysis and takes:** longer pieces on league math, FAAB strategy, and bye-week planning.
