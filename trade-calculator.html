Deep league fantasy football trade calculator

Pick the players on each side of a trade and see who wins it in a 14- or 16-team, full-PPR league. Values come from the Top 150 rankings, current through Week 8 (October 29, 2026).

Most trade calculators are built for 10- and 12-team leagues, where a bench player is always easy to replace. This deep league trade calculator uses the Top 150 fantasy football rankings for PPR deep leagues, so a Tier 2 starter is worth far more than two flex players, because in a 16-team league the second flex player is hard to replace. Add players to both sides, and the verdict updates as you go.

          How the trade values work

          Each player's value is worked out from his rank in the Top 150, on a curve that drops quickly at the top and flattens out. The number 1 player is worth 100 points, and the number 150 player is worth about 10. The curve was fitted to the shape of the Roto Street Journal's Week 8 full-PPR trade values, so it behaves the way published trade charts do.

          • Consolidation. If one side sends more players than the other receives, the extra players count at 60 percent. A deep roster can only start so many of them, and someone has to be dropped to make room.
          • Tier gap. This compares the best player on each side. Tiers are the same bands used on the Top 150 page, and a gap of two or more tiers usually matters.
